# Logging And Monitoring Policy

## Logged

- Authentication/security events.
- Guard decisions.
- Redacted risk categories.
- Billing events.
- Webhook/SIEM delivery status.
- Pilot activation events.

## Not Logged In Pilot Telemetry

- Raw prompts.
- Raw secrets.
- Raw tokens.
- Raw model inputs or outputs.

## Monitoring Evidence

Production monitoring proof is EVIDENCE REQUIRED before GA.
