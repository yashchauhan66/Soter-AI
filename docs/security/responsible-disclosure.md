# Responsible / Coordinated Disclosure — SoterAI

This page is the human-readable companion to `SECURITY.md` and
`/.well-known/security.txt`. If you have found a security issue in SoterAI /
Soter Guard, thank you — please report it privately.

## Report privately

- **Email:** `support@soterai.in` *(placeholder — replace with a monitored
  inbox before public launch)*
- **Do not** open a public GitHub issue or post the vulnerability publicly until
  we have had a chance to remediate.

See `SECURITY.md` for what to include, our SLA, safe-harbor terms, and the full
in-scope/out-of-scope list.

## What to expect

1. **Acknowledgement** within 2 business days.
2. **Triage + severity** within 5 business days (CVSS 3.1 guideline, adjusted for
   AI false-negative impact).
3. **Updates** at least every 7 days until resolved.
4. **Coordinated disclosure**, default 90 days from triage, sooner if a fix is
   deployed. Credit given unless you prefer anonymity.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research within the scope in `SECURITY.md` is authorized and will not
result in legal action. Do not access data beyond what is needed to demonstrate
the issue, and delete it after reporting.

## No bounty (yet)

There is no paid bounty program at this time; we acknowledge and credit valid
reports. Any future program will be announced in `SECURITY.md` and
`security.txt`.
