## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Accessible entry** — Free tier allows evaluation and small projects
2. **Transparent pricing** — No hidden fees, no usage surprises
3. **India-first** — Priced in INR for Indian market
4. **Scalable** — Plans grow with customer needs
5. **Enterprise flexible** — Custom pricing for large deployments
