# Positioning

**Date:** 2026-07-09

## One-Liner

SoterAI is an open, self-hostable AI security platform that protects prompts, data, agents, and RAG pipelines across every IDE, browser, and workflow tool.

## Positioning Statement

For development teams and enterprises building AI-powered applications, SoterAI is an AI security platform with unusually broad surface coverage — browsers, IDEs, APIs, RAG, and agent workflows in one place — plus native India/Hinglish support and a self-hosted option that keeps your data under your control. (We compare *breadth*, not "best detection"; see [`competitor-comparison.md`](competitor-comparison.md) and our honest efficacy numbers.)

## Key Messages

### Primary
- **Protect your AI journey from prompt to response** — SoterAI scans inputs, outputs, documents, and agent actions across every tool your team uses.

### Secondary
- **Your data stays yours** — Self-hosted option, no vendor lock-in, no data sent to third parties.
- **Built for India, works everywhere** — Native Hinglish and multilingual detection that most platforms in our comparison set do not document (as of 2026-07-10).
- **From individual developers to enterprise teams** — Free tier for developers, enterprise features for organizations.

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Breadth of coverage** — Browser + IDE + API + RAG + Agent
2. **India/Hinglish** — Native romanized-Hindi + multilingual detection, uncommon in our comparison set
3. **Self-hosted** — Data sovereignty
4. **Free tier** — Accessible entry point
5. **Honesty** — Transparent about capabilities and limitations

## What We Are NOT

- We do NOT claim "best detection" without external proof
- We do NOT replace your AI provider's built-in safety
- We do NOT store your prompt data (unless you choose cloud)
- We do NOT require vendor lock-in
