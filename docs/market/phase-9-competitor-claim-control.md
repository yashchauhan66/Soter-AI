# Phase 9 Competitor Claim Control

## Rule

Do not claim SoterAI beats a competitor unless the claim is measured on the same dataset, same labels, same threshold policy, and same runtime environment.

## Allowed Positioning

SoterAI's strongest differentiation is combining browser, IDE, API, n8n, MCP, and local-first AI workflow controls in one developer-friendly platform.

## Forbidden Positioning

- SoterAI is more accurate than a named competitor.
- SoterAI has the lowest false-positive rate.
- A competitor is unsafe or incomplete.
- SoterAI is best in world.
