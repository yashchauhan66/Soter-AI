## Decision and completed work

**SoterAI Secret Guard** is the selected visible name. It preserves the existing
SoterAI brand/domain and makes the secret-protection purpose clearer than Lite.
The canonical publisher/name remain `soterai.soterai-ide-guard`; no storage or
installation identity was renamed. Keywords now focus on the supported user
problem instead of a broad list of loosely related features and vendors.

## Rating and release recommendation

Keep the current engineering assessment at **6/10 overall**, **7/10 usefulness
within supported managed workflows**, and **4/10 stable-release readiness**.
These are subjective judgments. A clearer name and marketing kit do not supply
missing GUI, live-provider, configured upgrade/recovery or independent-user proof.

Prioritize frontend secret-exposure guidance, a proven protect-and-run workflow
for one stack, easier existing custom-secret registration, actual tool-route
verification and measured false-alarm/performance improvements. These suggestions
are detailed in the kit; they were not implemented as part of this marketing work.

Proceed with a closed pilot after same-candidate GUI/recovery smoke. Only use
public availability copy after checking the actual version/channel/target on
each registry. Stable publication remains unsupported by current evidence.
`releaseReady` remains false; no registry, social, email or website publication
was performed, and the local owner publishing wrapper remains disabled.

Public registry lookup could not be completed: sandbox network access failed,
automatic approval review timed out, and its permitted retry failed with the
technical error `content-blocked`. Name, domain and trademark availability have
not been independently established. No search-volume or ranking claims were
invented. The SEO/GEO skill was used for content structure and search intent;
its general visibility multipliers were not treated as project measurements.
