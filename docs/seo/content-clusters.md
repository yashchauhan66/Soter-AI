## Internal Linking Principles

1. Every pillar page links DOWN to all supporting pages in its cluster
2. Every supporting page links UP to its cluster pillar
3. Blog posts link to the most relevant product page (conversion CTA)
4. Comparison pages link to feature pages they compare
5. No page should be more than 3 clicks from the homepage
6. Benchmark evidence links are present on all feature pages
