# Choose your privacy mode

SoterAI Guard is **local-first**. By default every scan runs entirely on your machine — no code, prompts, or secrets leave your computer.

- **Local** (default) — 100% offline detection. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Hybrid** — local first; escalates only redacted, minimized payloads to the cloud, and only with your consent.
- **Cloud** — uses your configured SoterAI cloud endpoint for high-risk scans.

Press the button below to pick a mode. You can change it anytime in Settings.
