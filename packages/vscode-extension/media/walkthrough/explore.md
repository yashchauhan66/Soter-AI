# Explore the SoterAI Guard sidebar

Everything lives in the **SoterAI Guard** icon in the Activity Bar (left edge).

- **Project Risk** — a live risk score for your workspace.
- **Latest Findings** — what the most recent scan flagged.
- **Policy & Cloud Sync Status** — your active mode, policy, and connection state.

Two more things worth knowing:

- **Check Extension Health** (Command Palette) shows your current mode, trust, and policy at a glance — with no secrets in the report.
- Only 12 core commands show in the Command Palette by default. Turn on **`soterai.showAllCommands`** in Settings to reveal all advanced tools when you need them.

Press the button to open the sidebar. You're ready to go.
