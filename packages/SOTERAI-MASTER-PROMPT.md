**Priority Score =**

`(Security Impact × Customer Pain × Revenue Impact × Competitive Gap × Dependency Centrality × Evidence Strength)`

`÷`

`(Engineering Effort × Dependency Risk × Operational Burden)`

Score each factor from 1–5 and normalise the result to 100.

Selection rules:

1. Fix critical security or tenant-isolation issues first.
2. Fix broken builds, migrations and primary workflows next.
3. Complete foundational systems before dependent features.
4. Prefer components blocking paid customers or enterprise procurement.
5. Prefer high-value differentiation over low-value feature parity.
6. Do not select vanity features while P0 or P1 gaps remain.
7. Do not build a new service when an existing service should be fixed or simplified.
8. When two components have similar scores, select the one that unlocks more dependent components.

Record the selected component and reasoning in the report before making changes.

Do not ask the founder which component to select unless the choice requires a business decision that cannot be inferred safely.
