## 🔁 FOLLOW-UP RULES (burn these in)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| One bump only | A 2nd message after 4–5 days. A 3rd = spam. Stop. |
| Reply fast | If someone replies, respond <2 hours. Momentum converts. |
| Track everything | Copy the metrics tracker file 07's outreach tab. Know your reply rate by template. |
| Personalize first 2 lines | "Saw your talk at X" > any template. The first 2 lines decide opens. |
| Give before ask | Template, demo, free analysis — ALWAYS attach something useful that isn't "sign up". |
| Sundays > weekdays | For cold LinkedIn DMs to CTOs, Sunday 4–7 PM IST = less inbox competition. |
