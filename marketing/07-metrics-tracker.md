## 📈 DECISION RULES (when the numbers talk)

- **End of Week 1:** if a single channel produced >50% of new users → double its effort next week, cut the weakest channel.
- **End of Week 2 (post-launch):** if PH/HN drove a spike that decayed → normal. Keep Chrome/n8n/SEO (the compounds), treat launches as spikes on top.
- **End of Week 3:** if under 350 total → activate contingency: Day 22–27 push = 2 Reddit posts instead of 1, +10 outreach DMs, ask every existing user "who else should use this?" (referral ask — cheap, works).
- **Any week:** if something goes viral (a tweet, a Reddit post, a store ranking) — drop the calendar, ride that wave for 48 hrs. Momentum beats plans.
